About Us

DesignMantix is a global creative design, digital marketing, and publishing agency dedicated to helping businesses, brands, entrepreneurs, and authors build a powerful presence and grow worldwide.

We offer a complete range of creative and digital solutions, including graphic design, branding, website design and development, social media marketing, digital advertising, expert ghostwriting, professional book formatting, and captivating 3D cover designs.

As an end-to-end publishing studio, we help authors transform their ideas into professionally crafted books across eBook, Paperback, and Hardcover formats. We create publication-ready books for leading platforms such as Amazon KDP, Barnes & Noble, Apple Books, Google Play Books, and IngramSpark.

From building a brand and creating engaging digital experiences to writing, designing, and preparing books for publication, we manage every stage with creativity, strategy, technology, and attention to detail. Our team works with clients globally, delivering tailored solutions that help them stand out in today's competitive digital world.

At DesignMantix, we don't just create designs or publish books—we build brands, transform ideas into powerful experiences, and help our clients grow worldwide.
